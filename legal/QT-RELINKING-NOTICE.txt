QT DYNAMIC-LINKING AND MODIFICATION NOTICE

The official Vuttara Studio 0.0.1 Windows package installs Qt libraries and Qt
plugins as separate DLL files beside the application. Vuttara Studio does not
use a mechanism intended to prevent replacement of those libraries. A user may
inspect, replace, or debug compatible Qt library builds, subject to the
applicable Qt licences and technical compatibility requirements.

the Qt Project. See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt and the LGPLv3/GPLv3 texts in the
