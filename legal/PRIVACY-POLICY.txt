VUTTARA STUDIO PRIVACY POLICY
Effective date: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the official Vuttara Studio application and
related update and website services handle information. Vuttara Studio is
operated by Ward Reynolds in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

1. SUMMARY
Vuttara Studio 0.0.1 is primarily a local desktop application. It does not
include a Vuttara account, advertising, behavioural tracking, usage analytics,
automatic crash-report submission, cloud project storage, or cloud sync. The
application does not send recordings, projects, scenes, sources, logs, or
streaming credentials to Vuttara Studio.

Network activity occurs when you choose to stream to a third-party service,
when the application checks the official update feed, when you approve an
installer download from GitHub, or when you open an external website.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR COMPUTER
Depending on features you use, Vuttara Studio may store locally:
- application settings and window or dock layout;
- scenes, source configuration, transforms, visibility, and project state;
- recording and streaming settings;
- stream keys and service passwords encrypted for the current Windows user
  with Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI);
- local diagnostic and startup logs;
- downloaded update installers and temporary .part files;
- recordings in the directory you select; and
- other files you intentionally import, export, or create.

Local data remains under your control. Anyone with access to your Windows
account, backups, storage, or exported files may be able to access some of it.
DPAPI protection is not a substitute for a secure Windows account and device.

3. CAPTURE, RECORDING, AND STREAMING
Screen, window, camera, microphone, desktop-audio, and other source data is
processed on your computer. A recording is written to the local destination
you select. When you start streaming, encoded audio, video, and authentication
information are sent directly from your computer to the streaming server or
service you configured. Vuttara Studio does not operate an intermediary stream
relay in version 0.0.1.

The selected streaming provider may collect account, device, network, content,
and usage information under its own privacy policy. Review that provider's
policy before streaming.

4. UPDATE CHECKS AND DOWNLOADS
Stable builds may request:
https://vuttarastudio.nuttabuttainaa.com/updates/clean-rewrite/latest.json

The request includes ordinary network information such as your IP address and
a user-agent identifying the Vuttara Studio version and Windows x64 platform.
Website infrastructure may create routine security and access logs. When you
approve an update download, the application downloads the installer from the
official NutterButterInAA/vuttara-studio GitHub release path. GitHub receives
ordinary request and network information under GitHub's privacy policy.

The application verifies the published installer filename, file size, and
SHA-256 value before offering to run it. Vuttara Studio does not use update
requests for advertising or behavioural profiling.

5. WEBSITE AND HOSTING
The Vuttara Studio website may be delivered through hosting, content-delivery,
DNS, and security providers such as Cloudflare. Those providers may process IP
addresses, request headers, timestamps, security signals, and related log data
needed to deliver and protect the site. This policy does not replace their own
privacy notices.

6. INFORMATION VUTTARA STUDIO DOES NOT INTENTIONALLY COLLECT IN 0.0.1
The desktop application does not intentionally transmit to Vuttara Studio:
- recordings or live stream content;
- project or scene contents;
- camera, microphone, screen, or window captures;
- stream keys or streaming passwords;
- local application logs;
- contact lists or unrelated files;
- advertising identifiers; or
- analytics or telemetry events.

7. PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES
Information processed through official web and update infrastructure is used
to deliver requested content, provide updates, protect systems, diagnose
availability and security problems, prevent abuse, and comply with law. Where
applicable, processing is based on providing requested services, legitimate
security and operational interests, consent, or legal obligations.

8. RETENTION
Vuttara Studio does not centrally retain the local application data described
above. You control it through Windows and application settings. Website and
infrastructure logs may be retained by the relevant provider for security,
operations, troubleshooting, and legal compliance according to that provider's
practices. Update installers remain locally until you remove them or the
application replaces or cleans them.

9. SECURITY
The application uses HTTPS for update requests, restricts installer downloads
to the official GitHub release path, checks file size and SHA-256, and uses
Windows DPAPI for locally saved stream secrets. No system is perfectly secure.
Keep Windows updated, protect your account, use unique streaming credentials,
and verify official downloads.

10. INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING
GitHub, Cloudflare, streaming services, and other providers may process data in
Canada, the United States, or other countries. Their laws may differ from those
where you live.

11. CHILDREN AND TEENS
Vuttara Studio is recommended for ages 13 and older. A person below the age of
majority should use it with parent or guardian involvement. The desktop
application is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do
not stream or publish a minor's personal information without appropriate legal
permission and safeguards.

12. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS
You may use local recording features without configuring a streaming account.
You may decline an update download or installation. You may delete local
settings, logs, projects, recordings, and downloaded installers using the
application, Windows, or file management tools. Depending on where you live,
you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal
information held by an operator or service provider.

13. CHANGES
This policy may change when features, services, providers, or legal obligations
change. The effective date will be updated. A future account, cloud, analytics,
or crash-reporting feature would require an updated disclosure before release.
