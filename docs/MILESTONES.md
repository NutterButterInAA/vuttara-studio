# Development milestones

1. Foundation: clean Qt application, branding, logging and deployment.
2. Engine bootstrap: pinned libobs runtime, initialization and clean shutdown.
3. Preview: generated source rendered through libobs.
4. Capture: display, window and camera sources.
5. Scenes and transforms.
6. Audio mixer.
7. Recording.
8. Streaming.
9. Installer, legal pages and updater.

Every milestone must pass local validation before the next one begins.
